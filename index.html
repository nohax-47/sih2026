How a score is built

Prediction
XGBoost and LightGBM estimate cost overrun, time overrun and milestone success from each project's cost, timeline and progress data.
Delay analytics
Cox Proportional Hazards and Kaplan-Meier estimate delay hazard and survival probability, since timing risk needs a different model than a regression.
Explanation
SHAP breaks every score down into the specific features driving it, so a reviewer sees the cause, not just a number.
